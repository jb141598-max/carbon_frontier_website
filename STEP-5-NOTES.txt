CARBON FRONTIER WIKI — STEP 5
================================

This package includes every file from the previous build plus the complete
Step 5 page/media-management system.

INTERFACE CHANGES
-----------------
- Normal article text uses the Play font at regular (400) weight.
- History is hidden while reading and appears only after Edit Page is opened.
- Clicking the top search bar opens a centered search menu.
- The menu shows recently updated pages before a query is entered.
- Live results, arrow-key navigation, Enter to open, and Escape to close are supported.
- + New Page now lives inside the search menu.
- Staff can open recoverable Trash from the search menu.

STEP 5 FEATURES
---------------
- Upload PNG, JPG, WebP, or GIF images up to 4 MB from the editing toolbar.
- Private-wiki images are fetched through an authenticated Function and are not
  exposed by a public Blob URL.
- Move pages to a new address.
- The former address becomes a permanent wiki redirect automatically.
- Protect/unprotect an individual page from normal public contributors.
- Move pages to recoverable Trash without deleting revision history.
- Restore trashed pages from the staff Trash panel.
- The front page cannot be moved or trashed.
- Every page-management action is recorded in the wiki audit log.
- Cursor/Live Preview owner testing supports moves, redirects, protection,
  trash, restoration, and local image insertion without writing to production.

DEPLOYMENT
----------
Merge this folder into the website root, keeping the folder structure intact,
then deploy to Netlify. The new migration is applied during deployment.

New production files include:
- netlify/functions/wiki-media.mjs
- netlify/database/migrations/202608310001_add_wiki_page_management.sql

The existing CURSOR_TESTING_SYNC_KEY setting does not need to be changed.
