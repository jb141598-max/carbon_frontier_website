Private means assigned only

Owners, admins, and assigned wiki editors can always read and edit a private wiki. Unassigned visitors receive no private page content.

Protected role hierarchy

Owners appoint admins. Owners and admins appoint wiki editors. Admins cannot promote another admin or alter an owner.
