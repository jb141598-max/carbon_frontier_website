CARBON FRONTIER WIKI — STEP 6
================================

This package includes every file from the previous build plus the complete
Step 6 contributor-analytics system.

SEARCH INTERFACE FIXES
----------------------
- The page behind the search menu now uses a lighter gray overlay and a softer blur.
- The compact top search field keeps the normal text/I-beam mouse cursor.
- Clicking the compact field still opens the full search menu.
- The full search field is focused immediately, so typing can begin without a
  second click.

STEP 6 FEATURES
---------------
- Owners and Admins can open Analytics from the Wiki or Permissions top bar.
- Reports support exact inclusive start and end dates.
- “All Editors” includes every person who saved an edit in the selected range.
- “Assigned Wiki Editors” includes Owners, Admins, and assigned Wiki Editors.
- Assigned staff stay listed when they have zero edits.
- Unassigned people with zero edits are never listed.
- Summary cards show edits, pages created, active editors, and people listed.
- The report includes per-person edits, pages edited, pages created, and last edit.
- Email addresses are hidden by default and reveal/re-hide when clicked.
- The most-edited pages list respects the selected people filter.
- Contributor results can be downloaded as CSV.
- Cursor/Live Preview analytics combine the downloaded live wiki copy with local
  test edits and never write analytics data back to production.
- The production analytics Function independently verifies Google authentication
  and the Owner/Admin role before returning any report or email address.

DEPLOYMENT
----------
Merge this folder into the website root, keeping the folder structure intact,
then deploy to Netlify. Deployment is required for wiki-analytics.html,
wiki-analytics.js, the new Function, and the /wiki/analytics route to go live.

The existing CURSOR_TESTING_SYNC_KEY and Google settings do not need to change.
