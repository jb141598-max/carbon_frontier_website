CARBON FRONTIER WIKI — STEP 9
Reusable templates and visual Template Studio

WHAT WAS ADDED

- A reusable wiki template library at wiki-templates.html and /wiki/templates.
- A Google Drawings-style visual canvas for Wiki Editors, Admins, and Owners.
- Draggable and resizable text, placeholder text, rectangles, circles, frames,
  lines, and protected wiki images.
- Object properties for position, size, rotation, opacity, fonts, text color,
  alignment, fill, outline, corners, image fit, and layer order.
- Snap-to-grid, keyboard nudging, duplicate, delete, undo, and redo tools.
- Permanent independent template revisions with conflict protection.
- A starter Machine Infobox template.
- A Template button in article editing mode.
- A picker that asks for page-specific placeholder values before insertion.
- Double-click an inserted template while editing to change its values.
- Articles keep a revision-safe template snapshot, while normal reading loads
  the newest central template design so one design change updates every use.
- Live Testing snapshots now include wiki templates (snapshot schema v5).
- Template Studio works locally as the Cursor Owner and saves local-only edits.
- The redundant Back to Wiki control was removed from Permissions; the normal
  Wiki navigation button already returns to the front page.

DEPLOYMENT

Merge this folder into the website root and deploy once. The deployment is
required because Step 9 adds a database migration, a Netlify Function, a new
HTML/JavaScript editor page, and new routes. Netlify applies the template
migration automatically.

After deployment, local testing can use Refresh Live Data to download the live
template library without another deployment. Local edits remain local until the
files themselves are deployed or a change is saved on the production website.

IMPORTANT

The visual editor intentionally implements the practical wiki-template subset
of Google Drawings rather than pretending to reproduce every Google Drawings
feature. Its stored version-1 canvas format is extensible for future tools such
as grouping, connectors, tables, and richer vector paths.
